# Faux Foe: The Digital Lie Detector

## Introduction

Imagine receiving a video call from your CEO. You see their face, you hear their voice, and they urgently ask for a confidential transfer. You do it—only to realize an hour later that you just handed millions of dollars to an AI. We have entered an era where seeing is no longer believing. **Faux Foe** is here to bridge that gap. It is a real-time analysis engine designed to protect individuals and organizations from the growing threat of deepfake and impersonation frauds.

## Core Technology

To solve this, we didn't just build another detection tool. Introducing **Faux Foe**. At its core, Faux Foe is a real-time analysis engine. Whether it is a video conference or a standard phone call, our system analyzes the incoming stream instantly. It looks for artifacts that the human eye misses—glitches in pixel generation, voice synthesis errors, and unnatural micro-expressions.

### Key Features

- **Real-Time Detection:** Instant analysis of video and audio streams.
- **Non-Invasive User Experience:** Seamless integration into existing conference apps via a **plugin** or **Desktop App**.
- **Immediate Alerts:** Users receive a clear warning pop-up only when a threat is detected.
- **Centralized Threat Dashboard:** For IT Security Officers, monitor threat levels across the company in real-time.
- **Deployment Flexibility:** On-premise, hybrid, or cloud-based solutions tailored to customer needs.

## How to Use Faux Foe

### For End Users

1. **Install the Plugin/Desktop App:** Download and install Faux Foe from our website.
2. **Integration:** Connect Faux Foe to your preferred video conferencing app (e.g., Zoom, Teams, Google Meet).
3. **Automatic Monitoring:** Faux Foe runs in the background during your calls.
4. **Threat Alerts:** If a threat is detected, a warning pop-up will appear with actionable steps.

### For IT Security Teams

1. **Centralized Dashboard Setup:** Deploy the centralized dashboard to monitor threats across the organization.
2. **Custom Configuration:** Configure deployment options (on-premise, hybrid, or cloud) based on your organization's needs.
3. **Real-Time Alerts:** Receive notifications for coordinated attacks targeting multiple employees.
